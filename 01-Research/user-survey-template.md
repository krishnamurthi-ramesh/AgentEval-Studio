## Analysis Plan

| Question | What it measures | How to analyze |
|----------|-----------------|----------------|
| B2, B3 | Failure frequency & type distribution | Bar chart of error types; calculate error rate |
| B4 | Citation reliability perception | Distribution chart; cross-tab with B1 (verification behavior) |
| B5 | Trust level baseline | Mean, median; segment by usage frequency (A3) |
| C1 | Qualitative failure descriptions | Thematic coding into failure taxonomy |
| C3 | Topic-specific trust gaps | Ranked bar chart; compare with golden dataset coverage |
| D2 | Price of quality — willingness to switch | % who would switch; proxy for how much accuracy matters |

> **Target responses:** 40+ for statistical relevance
> **Expected completion time:** 5-7 minutes per respondent
